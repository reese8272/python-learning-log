1. **Q1** — walked through vigorously with interrogation: why choose that? how did you find that? how did you come up with it?
2. **Q2–3** — solved with checks.
3. **Q4–9** — solo in bulk.
4. **Q10** — solo.
5. **End** — AI reviewed the whole quiz after the fact.

Key auxiliary move: **constrain the search space** — ask the AI to name candidate *files*, then find the answers in the code himself. Narrows the search without outsourcing the finding.

The fade is the load-bearing part — support that never fades is a prosthetic; support that fades to zero is a teacher.
