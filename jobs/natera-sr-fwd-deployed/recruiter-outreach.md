## How To Open / Lead-In Guidance
- **Tone: peer-to-peer builder, not applicant begging.** Recruiters for this role screen for people who ship. Sound like one.
- **Lead with the running systems, not the résumé.** "I've built, deployed, and run..." beats "I have experience in..."
- **Reference the JD's own framing** — the "10–100x faster/more often than a human team" line — to show you read and understood what the role actually is.
- **Show, don't tell.** Put the GitHub and autoclip.studio links in every longer message. A live URL does more than three adjectives.
- **Do not lead with healthcare.** It's the gap; don't volunteer it in outreach. If asked, use the fast-ramp framing (see README Gaps To Frame).
