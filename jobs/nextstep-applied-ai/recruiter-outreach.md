## How to open the conversation
- Lead with "I already applied" — it signals you did the work and respects his pipeline.
- Name **both** roles in one breath so he can slot you wherever the client bites.
- State comp/remote/role-type up front so he can qualify you instantly. Recruiters move faster when you remove the guesswork.
- End with a question that puts the next step on him ("what do you need from me?").

## Follow-up cadence
- **Day 0:** Apply on SmartRecruiters (both) + send note.
- **Day 3–4:** If no reply, one short bump — "Following up on the Applied AI / Backend roles; still very interested, still remote + $120k+."
- **Day 8–10:** Final nudge, or ask if there's a better-fitting req on his desk. Then let it rest.
- If he engages: be responsive within hours, not days — recruiters reward speed.
