## How to open (principles)
- **Lead with production proof, not credentials.** First sentence = shipped work + a clickable link (autoclip.studio / GitHub). Let the artifact do the talking before the resume math kicks in.
- **Own the seniority gap early and honestly.** Naming it first ("~1 year, applying above tenure on the strength of scope") reads as confidence and removes the recruiter's biggest objection before they raise it.
- **Offer the down-level explicitly.** State you'd welcome a mid-level seat. This is the single move that keeps the door open when Senior is a no.
- **Mission tie-in, brief.** One line on care-at-scale — genuine, not fawning.

## Follow-up cadence
