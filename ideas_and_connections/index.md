| Bring the shiny thing to the audit BEFORE acting on it | The over-launch pattern's antidote isn't suppressing the surge — it's rerouting it. New standing rule: plans that appear after 10pm go to the next day's check-in, never into same-night action. | A cue isn't designed until it's physically installed — the tape counts, not the design session | Two "failed" habit cues (6am gym, water-on affirmations) turned out to have never failed: the physical welds (clothes laid out, alarm across the room, notecard taped in the shower) ran zero nights. Design sessions feel like progress but install nothing. Corollary from the same review: the calendar has never fired a habit for him — proven cues are object collisions in the room (phone hits charger → book is there). Every future cue needs an installation step with a date, and an installer ritual to run it (the 🌙 10:15 set-tomorrow slot). | Weekly review | 2026-07-27 |
| "It's easy, just do it" is the collapse voice; "I made showing up cheap" is the true one | Said at the anniversary dinner: "it's really easy to stick to these things." What's true is *engineered*: the brick survived a vacation because the cue was welded to the phone charger, not because of willpower. The honest sentence, evidence-backed: "I built something that makes showing up cheap, and when I use it, I move." | Reflection (4-year anniversary) | 2026-07-23 |
