# Ideas — 2026-04-08

**One problem journal.** Write one problem down in a notebook. Don't solve it. Let it sit. The brain keeps working on it in the background — incubation is real (psychologists call it that). The "ah-ha" comes when you weren't trying. First problem queued: *How do I build genuine desire?*

**Sleeping Beauty paradox (creativity block).** A woman is put to sleep, a coin is flipped, woken once (heads) or twice with memory erased between wakings (tails). Each time she wakes: "what's the probability it landed heads?" Debate: 1/2 (halfers — one fair coin, no new info) or 1/3 (thirders — tails produces more wakings, so heads is rarer among all wakings). The memory wipe is the core mechanism that makes it unsolvable from the inside. The "correct" answer may depend on whether you're counting experiments or wakings — genuinely unsettled.

**Smart people collect patterns, not facts.** Facts are noise without the pattern underneath. The CAREER_LOG patterns.md exists for exactly this reason — not to remember syntax, but to remember *why this over that*.

**What if instead of analyzing your life based on what others say, you based it on what YOU say?** Self-referential life review. The reflection system is already doing this — but this question makes it explicit.
