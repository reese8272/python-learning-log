# Ideas — 2026-04-09

**Desire as infrastructure, not willpower.** "How can I make my eating habit as dire and necessary as possible?" → connected to a bigger pattern: what are the desires that lead me to make changes? Better yet — what can I remind myself of to show the desire is already there? The desire doesn't need to be manufactured. It needs to be surfaced and made visible. This applies to eating, but also to the open problem: *How do I build genuine desire?*

**The feedback loop as a learning accelerator.** Read Carnegie, applied it five seconds later, asked for confirmation it worked. Read → apply → test → confirm. This is how knowledge becomes skill. Not studying, absorbing. Worth applying to other domains — what else can be tested in real time instead of just understood in theory?
