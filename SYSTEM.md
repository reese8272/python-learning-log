# SYSTEM.md — The Blueprint

*Rewritten 2026-08-27 for **Phase 3**. The 07-23 version was designed for a life with one engine (learning toward an exit) and a fixed clock (the peak window). That life is gone: the job now has a real project that teaches, the business has a real client feeding it real work, and the hours available on any given day are no longer predictable. This document is the constitution — the one place the whole machine is written down. Everything else (the Card, the tracker, CLAUDE.md, the protocols) is an instrument of this.*

**What this system promises, honestly:** not perfect days. **The downside of any failure is capped at one day, re-entry costs three minutes, and no day is ever illegitimate.** Upside is uncapped; variance is survivable; failure cannot compound. You don't win by never falling — you win because falling costs almost nothing and the way back is always the same door.

**What Phase 3 adds to that promise:** *a small day is now a declared, counted, first-class day.* The collapse pattern was never really about willpower. It was that the system had no legitimate way to say "today is a 20-minute day" — so a 20-minute day read as a failed day, and failed days compound. Phase 3 makes the declaration itself the behavior.

## Layer 0 — Identity & North Star (who / where)

Everything below exists to serve this. When priorities conflict, resolve upward to this layer.

- **The definite desire** (TAGR, surfaced 2026-04-30, unchanged): *generational wealth through AI positioning — knowledge as power, not trading time for money.*
- **The route changed (2026-07-25 → confirmed 2026-08-27).** It is no longer "escape to a $120k role." It is **two engines running at once**: grow inside Cognizant until the role is owned, and build the business until it can stand on its own. The `$6k rule` (every idle pipeline month costs ~$6k) is **retired** — it priced an urgency that no longer exists, and it was pricing the wrong thing.
- **The mastery bar** (unchanged): "Can I explain why THIS over THAT?" Syntax is handled; judgment is the product. Its Phase 3 sharpening: **the "why" of a decision is what it costs when it's wrong.**
- **Identity anchors:** full presence as a father and husband (the bad-day non-negotiable); *"I don't rely on easy — I engineered showing up."*
- **The four book-derived laws** (`readings/index.md`): intentionality beats volume · sincerity is the multiplier · failure is not defeat · small consistent deposits beat big gestures.

## Layer 2 — The Lanes (what the life is made of)

Phase 3's core structural change. **Life is four lanes.** Three of them can claim time; the fourth is derived and never competes for a block. Every rep belongs to exactly one lane, and the lane is named before the rep starts.

| Lane | Mission | One rep = | Scoreboard (the evidence it moved) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏡 **Home** | The ground everything else stands on — not a competitor to the other lanes | Presence, undistracted, for a real stretch | You were actually there. No log required. |
| 💼 **Craft** — Cognizant | Own the project. Be the person who performs *without* AI, visibly. | One **ownership touch**: predict a file/decision before opening it → find one non-obvious decision → answer ***what breaks if this is wrong*** → verify live against docs → one line logged | Standups, PRs, what you can defend cold, John's six notes |
| 🛠 **Business** — Ian + own products | Convert skill into money that isn't hours. | One **delivery touch**: a shipped slice, a client message sent, or a decision closed | Delivered work, client comms, invoices, live product |
| 🧠 **Depth** — learning | *Derived lane.* Runs on the residue of Craft and Business. | One **capture** (a thing you couldn't explain today, written down) or one `/drill` or one `/sharpen` | The Tripped list shrinking; skills defended cold |

**The Depth rule — this is the biggest change in Phase 3.** Learning no longer gets its own scheduled block, its own curriculum, or its own daily obligation. **All four curriculum tracks are formally paused** (`api`, `ai`, `py`, `soft` — 4 banked of 233 since April; see `DECISIONS.md` 2026-08-27). They stay on disk as a **reference catalog**, not a backlog. The engine that replaces them:

> **Work generates the curriculum now.** Every Craft rep and every Business rep throws off something you couldn't explain. That goes in the capture line. `/drill` and `/sharpen` walk the captures. `/learn new <topic>` is available on demand when a capture is big enough to need teaching — driven by what work threw at you, never by a roadmap position.

This is not a downgrade. The record is explicit: 17 `/learn` sessions produced 4 banked units, while the *one* time learning visibly converted to career movement (07-28 → 07-29, the work quiz then the boss call) it was a **defense** rep on code he didn't write, under real stakes. Phase 3 stops manufacturing stakes and uses the real ones.

## Layer 3 — The Router (how a day gets decided)

**There is no peak window and no fixed daily protocol.** Both are retired by decision (2026-08-27, Reese's call: *"I don't want a peak window — I need dynamic scheduling based on the needs of the day"*). The 9:45 block also sat inside client hours, which John's review explicitly flagged.

> The nightly build (`SCHEDULER.md`) writes a proposal for tomorrow into the calendar. It is **not** a protocol and not a grid: it caps at three blocks, it is computed fresh from tomorrow's real commitments, and it has no authority. The route ratifies it or discards it. A plan `/today` overrules is a plan working correctly.

*Adopted at the 2026-09-08 audit with two amendments the audit imposed on its own spec: the kill condition is now a **start** condition (`SCHEDULER.md` §9), and the run must sweep stale auto-events rather than let them accumulate (§5, guardrail 9). Both exist because an unratified proposal is not free — it is a clock cue you are learning to ignore, which is the mechanism that killed fixed clock-time cues on 08-27.*

What replaces them is **`/today` — the route.** 60 seconds. Phone-friendly. Fixed cue, dynamic content.

> **The principle: don't schedule the time, schedule the decision.**
> A fluid schedule fails when "fluid" quietly becomes "never decided." The route is the fixed part. What it produces changes every single day.

**The route, in three moves:**

1. **Declare the day type.** Not discovered at 9pm — *declared*, out loud, at the cue.
2. **Pick the lane** that needs it most today. One lane gets the rep. Not all three.
3. **Name the one thing** — specific enough that starting requires no further decision.

### The three day types

| Type | Shape | What's owed | What it counts as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟩 **Deep** | A real block exists (60–90+ min), energy is there | Floor + **one deep rep** in the chosen lane | A great day |
| 🟨 **Split** | Fragments only — meetings, kids, errands, low battery | Floor + **one 15–20 min rep** in the chosen lane | A completed day |
| 🟥 **Survival** | Obligations own the day, or the tank is empty | **Floor only.** The rep is explicitly *none*. | **A completed day.** Full stop. |

**🟥 is not failure — it is a legitimate move, and using it correctly is a skill.** The single most important sentence in this document: *a declared 🟥 day counts, and a 🟥 day never carries debt into tomorrow.* The all-or-nothing collapse (six recorded instances: 4/09, 5/01, 6/16, Jul 11–19, Jul 30–Aug 3, Aug 12–17) happens in the gap between "I can't do the full thing" and "so I did nothing and felt bad about it." 🟥 closes that gap by making the small day a *choice* instead of a *failure*.

**The one guard:** three 🟥 days in a row is not a crisis and not a moral problem — it is a **signal the audit reads**. It means either life is genuinely heavy (fine, note it and continue) or a lane has quietly gone dark (fixable). `/audit` catches it. Nothing else does anything about it.

## Layer 4 — The Floor (the minimum viable day)

Simplified for Phase 3 to two items, both lane-agnostic:

- **A day counts when: 🧱 the brick happened, and the day's declared rep happened** — where a 🟥 day's declared rep is *none*, so 🟥 + brick = a counted day.
- **Weekends owe nothing professional** (Reese's rule, 07-25). Weekend floor = the brick, or full presence with the kids. Any Craft or Business touch on a weekend is a bonus, never owed.
- **Bad-day rule:** ONE non-negotiable keeps the identity — full presence with the kids, or the brick. That day still counts.
- **Re-entry rule:** after any gap, the way back is `/checkin`. Gaps are data, never debt. No archaeology, no make-up sessions.
- **The Card** (`agenda.md`) is the front door and must always reflect NOW. A stale Card is a system bug.

The brick stays the floor's anchor because it is the single most-proven behavior in the entire log — it survived a full vacation with zero scaffolding (07-13 → 07-19) and every collapse since April has ended with it.

## Layer 5 — The Audit (the layer Phase 3 is actually built around)

This is the answer to *"I need to audit the system and habits heavily."* It is also the answer to a problem the 08-18 session named with numbers: **28 system/card commits against 17 learn, 2 sharpen, 2 drill since 06-01.** System-tinkering is the most sophisticated procrastination available here, because from the inside it is indistinguishable from progress.

So the audit is not "more system." It is the **valve that turns the tinkering impulse into a scheduled, bounded, subtractive event.**

### The Change Window rule — enforce this above almost everything

> **The system may only be changed during an `/audit`.**
> Outside an audit, a system idea — a new command, a new file, a restructure, a renamed block, a better tracker — goes into `PARKING.md` as one line and is not built. Not that day, not that night, not "while I'm in here anyway."

This is the over-launch counter with teeth. It costs nothing to obey (one line in a file) and it removes the only mechanism by which this system has ever eaten itself.

### `/audit` — the five passes

Runs on **evidence, not memory**. Weekly during the Phase 3 transition, then monthly once the lanes are stable.

1. **Evidence pass** — count it before opining. Commits by type, days since each lane was last touched, floor hit rate, day-type distribution (🟩/🟨/🟥), streak reality. Numbers only, no verdicts yet.
2. **Lane pass** — each lane gets a one-word verdict: **Moving · Stalled · Dark**, with the evidence cited. A Dark lane is a finding, not a failure.
3. **Habit pass** — every habit gets a verdict, and one of the options is the sharpest diagnostic this system has ever produced: **Installed · Installing · Never installed · Retired.** *"Never installed"* is not the same as *"failed"* (07-27: zero nights of night-before gym setup, no notecard in the shower — both were called failures and neither had ever been tried). A cue isn't designed until it is physically installed; the tape counts, not the design session.
4. **Subtraction pass — the audit must retire at least one thing.** A rule, a file, a counter, a habit, a block, a doc. Nothing is grandfathered by having once been a good idea. If nothing can be cut, the audit isn't finished looking.
5. **The one change** — at most **one** addition, and only if pass 4 already cut something. One in, one out, minimum.

Every audit writes its verdicts to `habits/tracker.md` and its decisions to `DECISIONS.md`, and empties `PARKING.md` (each line: built, parked again, or killed — never silently carried).

## Layer 6 — Feedback loops

`/today` (the route, 60 sec) → `/checkin` (daily floor, 3 min) → `/reflect` (when there's something to process) → `/weekly-review` (game film) → `/audit` (the system and habits, on evidence) → `/monthly-review` (the arc).

Content survives the **3-iteration filter** (daily → weekly → monthly) or it wasn't load-bearing. Design changes land in `DECISIONS.md`; habit truth lives in `habits/tracker.md`; coaching memory in Coach Notes.

## The Cue Map — every behavior, its trigger, its floor

*The core mechanism: no behavior relies on remembering or wanting. Every one is an if-then wired to something that already happens, with a worst-day minimum. Cue failure → re-graft the cue; never indict willpower.*

**Phase 3 change: the cues are anchored to transitions, not clock times.** Clock anchors were the right design when the day had a predictable shape. It doesn't anymore, and a clock cue that fires during a client meeting teaches you to ignore cues.

| Behavior | Cue (if → then) | Worst-day minimum |
|---|---|---|
| **The route** | First sit-down at the desk (or first coffee) → `/today`, 60 seconds | Declare the day type. That alone is the route. |
| **The rep** | The route named it → start it, timer on | 10 minutes of the named thing |
| **Craft ownership touch** | Before the workday close-out → one file, one decision, one failure mode | One line: *file · decision · what breaks if wrong* |
| **Business touch** | Kids down → one message sent or one commit pushed | The message. Sent beats perfect. |
| **Capture (Depth)** | Anything you couldn't explain today → one line, immediately | The line itself |
| **System idea** | Idea appears → `PARKING.md`, **not** the editor | The line. Building it is the failure. |
| **Movement** | Whatever already gets you out of the chair → 20 min | Ten minutes, any form |
| **The brick** | Phone on charger FIRST → book in hand, before horizontal | A page |
| **Gratitude close** | Lights out → 3 things | One thing |
| **Night-before setup** | Brick finished → clothes out, alarm across the room | Clothes out |
| **Re-entry after any gap** | Notice the gap → `/checkin`, nothing else | The 3 minutes |

**After 10pm: no launches.** A big plan appearing after 10pm goes to `PARKING.md` and the next route — never into same-night action. (Proven three times: certs 07-20, contractor platform 07-23, and every collapse that started with a midnight rebuild.)

## The Failure-Mode Playbook — every known collapse, pre-answered

*These are the ways this system has actually died, each with its engineered counter. When something feels broken, find the row — the answer is already written.*

| Failure mode | Signature | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| **All-or-nothing collapse** | External hit → whole stack down → guilt blocks re-entry for weeks (4/09, 5/01, 6/16, Jul 11–19, Jul 30–Aug 3, Aug 12–17 — **six instances**) | 🟥 makes the small day legitimate *in advance*; the Floor caps the day's cost; `/checkin` re-enters in 3 min; gaps are data, never debt |
| **System-tinkering as progress** | Building the container feels identical to doing the work from the inside (28 system commits : 17 learn : 2 sharpen : 2 drill, since 06-01) | **The Change Window.** System edits happen only inside `/audit`; ideas go to `PARKING.md`. Every audit must subtract. |
| **Over-launch** | Surge of motivation → rebuild everything at once → nothing sticks (the signature of every collapse) | One build at a time; new bricks only after the current one is Installed; an audit adds at most one thing and only after cutting one |
| **The fluid-schedule trap** *(new, Phase 3)* | "Dynamic" quietly becomes "never decided"; the day ends without a rep and without a reason | The route is a **fixed cue with dynamic content**. The declaration is non-negotiable; the time is not. A day with no declaration is the only real miss. |
| **Lane blur** *(new, Phase 3)* | Work bleeds into business bleeds into evening; three lanes touched, none moved, all day felt busy | One lane per rep, named *before* starting. Lanes are audited separately so a Dark lane can't hide behind a busy one. |
| **Client-load creep** *(new, Phase 3)* | Paid, externally-demanded work expands to fill every hour, because it's the only lane with someone else waiting | The Business lane gets a **declared weekly ceiling**, set at the audit. Above the ceiling is a scope conversation with the client — never a silent absorption. |
| **Cue eviction** | A habit "falls" because life moved its trigger (midday gym killed by work; the 9:45 block killed by a real project) | Diagnose the cue, not the discipline. Re-graft to a transition that still happens, as a *new build*. |
| **Never-installed-vs-failed** | A habit is judged a failure when its cue was never physically installed (07-27: zero setup nights, no notecard) | The audit's habit pass forces the distinction. The tape counts, not the design session. Install within the minute of the thought. |
| **Adjacent-habit swap** | Build feels heavy → a smaller nearby habit quietly replaces it (stretching nearly replacing gym, 07-20) | Invoke the real build's worst-day minimum instead |
| **Judgment-exposure spiral** | Rejection / being watched reads as a verdict → "I don't know enough" | The fear vanishes where no one's watching — proof it isn't skill. Curiosity over ranking. |
| **States the rule, not the reason** | Recall is strong, justification is absent (four occurrences, 08-18) | When asked "why," the first sentence may not contain the thing being explained. **Grade the second sentence.** |
