# business/ — prompts and tone

*The 🛠 Business lane: Ian's contract work and own products. Created 2026-08-27 with Phase 3.*

## What goes here

- **`LEDGER.md`** — the living scoreboard: clients, weekly ceiling, active work, what's owed, what shipped. Update on **commit / deliver / renegotiate**, not on every touch.
- **`reflection_log/YYYY-MM-DD.md`** — dated raw notes: client sessions, delivery notes, scope conversations, the thinking behind a pricing or scoping call.

## Prompts for this lane

- What did I actually **deliver** this week — not work on, deliver?
- What did I commit to out loud, and to whom?
- Is anything **owed** that I haven't sent? (An owed message is worse than a late delivery.)
- Did this week's work move the business, or just fill it? Hours billed is not the same as a business built.
- Where did I trade time for money when I could have built something that doesn't?
- Did the work exceed the weekly ceiling? If so — was that a choice, or did it just happen?

## Tone

Same as everywhere in this repo: process praise not trait praise, "and" not "but", gaps are data never debt.

Lane-specific: **this is the lane where the North Star actually lives** — *generational wealth through AI positioning, not trading time for money.* Contract work is the bridge, not the destination. When contract hours crowd out product work for weeks running, that's worth naming — and it is a **strategy** observation, never a discipline one.

## The three questions this lane exists to answer

1. **Is anything owed?** (checked every session)
2. **Did something ship?** (checked every audit)
3. **Is the ceiling holding?** (set and checked every audit)
