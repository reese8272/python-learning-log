Each book gets its own folder. Dated entries capture raw notes as you read. `summary.md` is the master file — your take on the whole book, built up over time.

- What's the core idea of this book in your own words?
- What challenged or surprised you in what you read today?
- Where does this connect to something already happening in your life?
- What would you tell someone about this book in two sentences?
- Is there a passage or idea worth pulling into `summary.md`?
