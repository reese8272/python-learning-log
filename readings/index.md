## Patterns

### Intentionality beats volume
- **TAGR:** Desire must be DEFINITE — a burning desire, not a vague wish
- **Life is Short:** Write everything you want, circle top 3, everything else goes on the avoid list
- **HTWFAIP:** The one question that changes everything: what do THEY want?
- **Thread:** Know exactly what you're pursuing, what you're ignoring, and who you're talking to. Precision over activity.
- **Update (2026-04-30):** The TAGR "definite desire" was an open question for weeks. It surfaced in a gratitude: "I am in the AI space and primed to be the next generation of wealth — knowledge as power, not trading time for money." That's the written-down specific version Hill was asking for.

### Sincerity is the multiplier
- **HTWFAIP:** Appreciation vs. flattery — people feel the difference. Genuine interest > social tactics.
- **Life is Short:** Unexpressed gratitude is like losing money. Deposits > presence.
- **TAGR:** You sync with your dominating thoughts — if the desire isn't real, nothing downstream works.
- **Thread:** You can't fake the fundamentals. Real intention, real interest, real gratitude — these compound. Performed versions decay.

### Failure is not defeat
- **TAGR:** Three feet from gold — the logical move is to quit, but that's one step before the breakthrough
- **Life is Short:** You need bad ideas to clear the way for good ones. Constraints make you creative.
- **Thread:** The people who succeed are the ones who kept going past the point where quitting made sense. The reflection system itself is proof — five years of failed attempts, and this one held.

### Small consistent deposits beat big gestures
- **Life is Short:** Relationships are deposits or withdrawals. Small daily deposits > grand gestures.
- **HTWFAIP:** Put yourself out for others — time, energy, selflessness, thoughtfulness
- **Life is Short:** Tiny adjustments make massive results. Monthly reviews prevent yearly regrets.
- **Thread:** The gym streak, the morning stack, showing up for the kids — none of these are dramatic. They're just consistent. That's the whole game.
