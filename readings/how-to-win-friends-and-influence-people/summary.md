# How to Win Friends and Influence People
*Dale Carnegie*

**Status:** In progress

---

## What kind of book is this

A practical guide to human relationships — less about manipulation, more about genuinely understanding people. The core insight is that most conflict and friction comes from people being too focused on their own perspective to ever see anyone else's.

---

## Major Ideas

### See it from their angle first
To get someone to do something, you have to understand what they want — not just what you want. Most people skip this entirely and wonder why nothing works. The person who can genuinely flip to the other perspective has an enormous advantage in every relationship.

> *"It's a miracle what can be of someone who looks at things from someone else's point of view."*
