# How to Win Friends and Influence People
*Dale Carnegie*

**Status:** In progress

---

## What kind of book is this

A practical guide to human relationships — less about manipulation, more about genuinely understanding people. The core insight is that most conflict and friction comes from people being too focused on their own perspective to ever see anyone else's.

---

## Major Ideas

### Don't criticize (Ch. 1)
Criticism doesn't work. People don't criticize themselves no matter how wrong they are — they rationalize, defend, self-impose. Criticism makes people dig in, not change. The move is to ask "what can we do better?" instead of "why did you do that?" Check yourself first.

### Appreciation is the deepest currency (Ch. 2)
The deepest craving in human nature is the craving to be appreciated — genuinely, not flattered. People work harder and go further under sincere appreciation than under any amount of pressure or criticism. Flattery is hollow; real appreciation requires actually seeing the person.

### See it from their angle first (Ch. 3)
To get someone to do something, you have to understand what they want — not just what you want. Most people skip this entirely and wonder why nothing works. The person who can genuinely flip to the other perspective has an enormous advantage in every relationship.

> *"It's a miracle what can be of someone who looks at things from someone else's point of view."*
