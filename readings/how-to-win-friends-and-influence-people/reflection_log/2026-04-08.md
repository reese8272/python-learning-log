## Section 2, Ch 1 — Do This and You'll Be Welcome Anywhere

**Main Idea:** You can make more friends in two months by becoming genuinely interested in other people than you can in two years by trying to get people interested in you. Genuine interest, not performed interest.

- You can make more friends in two months by becoming genuinely interested in people than in two years by trying to get them interested in you
- The individual not interested in his fellow men has the greatest difficulties in life — all human failures spring from among such people
- All of us like people who admire us
- Put yourself out to do things for others — things that require time, energy, selflessness, and thoughtfulness
- If we want to make friends, greet people with animation and enthusiasm
- We are interested in others when they are interested in us
- Genuine interest might allow you to get what you want without asking
- Acknowledging someone's interest *outside* the moment you talked about it is huge (e.g., notice someone hasn't seen a bird before — call days later to say "I think that bird is outside your window right now")
- An enthusiastic "Hi" when you pick up the phone goes a long way
- Even small interests spark big opportunities
